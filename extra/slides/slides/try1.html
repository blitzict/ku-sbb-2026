try1 · the idea

Measure both halves and compare

Cut each series at the marked point. Describe the “before” half and the “after” half with many measurements, then look at the differences.

Level

Is the average higher or lower after the point?

Spread

Do the values swing more widely?

Shape

Do the two halves look like they come from the same mix of values?

Memory

Does today’s value still predict tomorrow’s as well as before?
