try2 · the idea

Ask how the numbers were produced

try1 asked “do the halves look different?”. try2 also asks “did the process making the numbers change?” I fit simple descriptions of that process to each half and compare them.

01

Rhythm

a repeating cycle that changes speed or stops

02

Trend

a steady climb that bends or reverses

03

Jumps

sudden spikes appearing or vanishing

04

Calm vs stormy

quiet and noisy periods switching

05

Noise

the random part getting louder or changing type

06

Momentum

how strongly one value pulls the next
