Steps 1 and 2 give the 0.879 ensemble (local 0.87910, public 0.88077). Step 3: a 5-fold MLP on 361 label-free measurements, trained on the real training series plus 25,977 simulated series from the ‘v3 simulator’: real no-break training series (donors, only from that fold’s training split) with a break injected, so their label is known. Each simulated series counts 1/20 as much as a real one (weight 0.05). A 5-coefficient correction then mixes its logit into the ensemble. Effect: public 0.88077 → 0.88078, local 0.87911 → 0.87910, i.e. within noise. Caveat from my report: the simulator's break-size settings came from research that used all training labels.
