The task

Each example is a time series: a list of numbers recorded one after another, like a daily temperature or a machine’s sensor reading.

Each one has a marked point. The question: did the behaviour change at that point? That change is called a structural break.

I get 24,000 practice series with the answer, and must give a probability for 4,000 new ones.
