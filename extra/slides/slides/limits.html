Limitations

What try4 can’t do

Quiet breaks slip through

43% of breaks that move the average or spread by under 0.15 SD land in the bottom half.

Short series are harder

0.82 on the shortest third (256–512 points) against 0.91 on the longest.

Nerfed by the rules

The 30-minute limit costs about 0.005: 0.876 for try4 against 0.881 for try3.

Picks aren’t “the truth”

Only 39 of the 100 columns are picked in all 5 rounds. Many stand in for each other, so read rankings loosely.

A ranking, not a probability

The 3 models’ ranks are averaged, so the output orders series but isn’t a calibrated chance, and all test series are scored in one run.

Built for this data

Designed around these series, untested on other data. A 4,000-series score also wobbles by about ±0.005.
