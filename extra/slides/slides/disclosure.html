Environment, sources and AI use

What it ran on, and who helped

01

Environment

Python 3.12 · LightGBM 4.7.0 · XGBoost 3.4.1 · scikit-learn 1.9.1 · numpy 2.5.4 · pandas 3.1.0 · scipy 1.18.1, pinned in requirements-cpu-full.txt. Trained on my own PC; scored on Kaggle with 2 CPU cores and 8 GB.

02

Sources

Only the competition’s training files. No outside data, no pretrained models, no test labels. try3’s correction network also used made-up series built from training no-break series.

03

AI use

Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) turned my ideas into code, sped up training on my 2 GPUs, ported the features to a CPU-only run, and helped build these slides and the scripts behind their numbers.
